LUT to recipe

Turn a colour LUT into a recipe file for the GR II. The camera has no 3D-LUT stage, so the converter fits the stages it has (colour matrix, hue sectors, tone curves) to your LUT and tells you how close it gets. Everything runs in this page; your file never leaves your computer.

    Credit and licence of the LUT (optional)

    Copy the file to cfw/recipes/ on the card, choose DEV → Reload recipes (or restart), and pick it in Recipes.

    What the converter can and cannot do
    • It fits a colour matrix, 12 hue-sector matrices, three tone curves and a post matrix, relative to the camera's Standard rendering, and writes them as the recipe's look keys with base=setting2. The camera's saturation, contrast and sharpness sliders still work on top.
    • It models the Standard rendering with its own approximation, so the result is close but not exact; the colour difference (CIEDE2000) is measured on held-out colours. Below about 2 is close; above 5 the LUT does something the camera cannot follow.
    • No grain, halation or blur, and no colour-dependent tone changes: those are not part of the camera's colour path.
    • Log LUTs (V-Log, S-Log3, LogC3, F-Log, F-Log2) are fed scene light through the matching log curve and gamut and taken as Rec.709 out.
    • The recipe file follows the recipe file format: ASCII, CRLF, 8.3 file name, under 4 KB.
