GR II TTY own recipe designs

rangefnd.txt (Rangefinder), mono_hc.txt (Mono Hard), mono_sep.txt (Mono Soft Sepia) and
mono_psh.txt (Mono Pushed Street) are own designs of the GR II TTY project. Each look is a parametric
target written by the project (characteristic curve on log exposure, colour-filter mix for black
and white, sepia toning, hue-dependent chroma and hue changes in OKLab; every parameter is listed in
the file's header) and fitted to Ricoh GR II settings relative to the camera's Standard rendering
through a model of its look stages. They contain no third-party data: no film profile, LUT, Ricoh
table or other recipe.
