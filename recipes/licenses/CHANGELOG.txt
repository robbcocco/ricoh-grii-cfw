Changes to the spektrafilm material (as asked by SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt and CC BY-SA 4.0)

No LUT, profile, spectral data or rendered image from spektrafilm is included in this repository.
Each spektrafilm-derived recipe file encodes the look of one spektrafilm film/print simulation (or,
for Vivid Slide, of a scanned reversal film) as Ricoh GR II camera settings relative to the camera's
Standard rendering: a 3x3 colour matrix, three 33-knot tone curves, a 3x3 post matrix and twelve
3x3 hue-sector matrices. The own designs (Rangefinder, Mono Hard, Mono Soft Sepia, Mono Pushed
Street) use no spektrafilm material; see OWN_DESIGNS.txt.

Source: spektrafilm 0.3.4 by Andrea Volpato, https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm, commit
3bb2c2d2801ff68b92019cf1dbcbb133d60832bc; film and print profiles CC BY-SA 4.0. Profiles used
(film / print): kodak_portra_400, kodak_ektar_100, kodak_gold_200, fujifilm_xtra_400,
kodak_ultramax_400, kodak_vision3_50d / kodak_portra_endura; fujifilm_pro_400h /
fujifilm_crystal_archive_typeii; kodak_vision3_250d, kodak_vision3_500t / kodak_2383; Cinema Green:
kodak_vision3_500t / kodak_2383; Cinema Yellow: kodak_vision3_250d / kodak_2393; Cinema Warm:
kodak_vision3_250d / kodak_2383; Vivid Slide: fujifilm_velvia_100 (reversal film, scanned); 800T and
800D (halation test recipes): kodak_vision3_500t / kodak_2383 and kodak_vision3_250d / kodak_2383.

Method (gr2_cfw project, its spektrafilm fitting tool): spektrafilm's own runtime
(init_params, digest_params, simulation pipeline in its deterministic LUT mode: no grain, halation,
blur or auto exposure; database neutral print filters) renders synthetic scene-linear sRGB samples
at its nominal exposure: a dense neutral ramp, ColorChecker 24 patches and an sRGB grid at several
exposures. A model of the GR II look stages (Standard colour matrix, hue sectors, tone curves,
RGB->YCbCr) is fitted to those renders by least squares in CIELAB: the tone curves from the neutral
ramp, then the matrices and hue sectors. The fitted values are rounded to the recipe integers.
The cinema looks are print-timed inside spektrafilm, with its own runtime parameters (all others
at their defaults), listed in each recipe header. Cinema Green: enlarger magenta filter +10 CC and
print exposure 1.1, print density-curve morph gamma 0.85 on the magenta layer and 0.75 on the slow
(shoulder) sub-layers, DIR interlayer inhibition 0.6. Cinema Yellow: enlarger yellow filter -12 CC,
print density-curve morph gamma 0.8 on the yellow layer and 0.7 on the slow sub-layers, DIR
interlayer inhibition 0.65. Cinema Warm: enlarger yellow filter -5 CC, magenta filter +2 CC, print
exposure 1.04, print density-curve morph gamma 0.82 on all layers and 0.85 on the slow sub-layers,
DIR interlayer inhibition 0.9.
Vivid Slide renders the reversal film itself with spektrafilm's film scan (io.scan_film; no print
stage, no scanner white/black correction in LUT mode), otherwise as above.
800T and 800D carry the looks of the Vision3 500T and 250D recipes plus two halation keys:
spektrafilm's back-reflection halation of the same film profile with its anti-halation class set to
"no" (rem-jet removed), as spektrafilm's own parameter builder (digest_params) gives it: the strength
per channel and the first-bounce size; the gr2_cfw firmware renders the halo with spektrafilm's
halation model (three bounces, decay 0.5, renormalised) on the developed photo.
Modified by the gr2_cfw project; the spektrafilm-derived recipe files are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
